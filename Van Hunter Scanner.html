Infrared test

Point a TV remote at the lens and press a button. If you see a purple or white glow on screen, that camera picks up near infrared. Try both cameras. iPhone front cameras often show it, rear cameras usually do not.

Web pages cannot use Face ID's infrared sensor or LiDAR. For real infrared, add an 850 nm infrared LED light and an 850 nm pass filter over a camera that passed the test, and select Brightness above.
